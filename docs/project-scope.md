# MANÁ Project Scope

MANÁ is a business management web application for a small bakery in Monterrey, Mexico.

The first version will focus on the bakery's three main priorities:

1. See the monthly business result.
2. Schedule and manage orders.
3. Track costs and expenses.

## In Scope for the First Version

The first version will include:

- Product management.
- Customer management.
- Order creation and editing.
- Order status tracking.
- Upcoming order visibility.
- Delivery method and delivery fee tracking.
- Expense recording.
- Monthly sales totals.
- Monthly expense totals.
- Recorded business result calculated as recorded sales minus recorded expenses.
- Basic sales reporting by product.

## Out of Scope for the First Version

The first version will not include:

- Ingredient inventory.
- Recipe costing.
- Product margin analysis.
- Advanced accounting or tax features.
- Deposits or online card payments.
- WhatsApp or Twilio integration.
- Customer self-service portal.
- AI features.
- Microservices or other unnecessary distributed architecture.
